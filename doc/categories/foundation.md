Design tokens, theming and app-level services that every other layer builds on.

Includes `OiApp`, `OiThemeData` with its token groups (`OiColorScheme`, `OiTextTheme`, `OiSpacingScale`, `OiRadiusScale`, `OiShadowScale`, `OiAnimationConfig`), the responsive helpers (`OiBreakpointScale`, `OiResponsive`), `OiIcons`, and settings persistence through `OiSettingsDriver`.
